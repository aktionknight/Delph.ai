Channel guidance: Instagram.
Adapt the brand voice to a warm, conversational and visually led register for the supplied audience. Use an immediate, accessible hook, short sentences and natural everyday phrasing. Avoid corporate announcement language, long business essays, inflated promises and forced slang. Emojis and a few relevant hashtags are optional when they fit the brand; do not force them.
Write a publishable caption with a concise opening and a relevant CTA, distinct from production directions or a spoken script. Choose a natural invitation to explore, save, share or reply that fits the timeline objective; do not invent urgency. Reels should feel speakable and paced for short vertical video. Carousels need a clear progression across slides; stories need brief frame-friendly copy; static posts need concise, visually complementary copy.
For image plans, favor a strong focal point, readable minimal text and a composition suited to the selected Instagram format while preserving the brand palette. For voiceover, use natural spoken language and omit hashtags, shot directions and caption text. Apply these choices to repairs without inventing claims or overriding factual grounding and human feedback.
