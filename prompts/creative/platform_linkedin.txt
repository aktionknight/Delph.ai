Channel guidance: LinkedIn.
Adapt the brand voice to a professional, approachable register for the supplied audience. Lead with a relevant work observation or clear, supported idea. Explain business relevance in concise paragraphs with a logical progression; prefer concrete language over jargon. Avoid exaggerated hype, influencer slang, forced emojis and engagement bait. Do not invent business benefits, ROI, testimonials or metrics to sound professional.
Use a thoughtful, specific CTA that fits the timeline objective, such as exploring the documented workflow or inviting a relevant discussion. Keep the hook, body and CTA one coherent post. Caption should be empty unless explicitly requested as an alternative; do not duplicate the post in another field.
For image plans, favor clear editorial composition, restrained typography, legibility and the brand palette. Let the documented idea drive the visual; do not fabricate product screens or charts. Apply these channel choices to new drafts and repairs while honoring factual grounding, brand voice and human feedback.
